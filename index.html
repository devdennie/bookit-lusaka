About

PamoHub in this repository is a static demo. It is not a live marketplace. There is no account system, no payment and no campus partnership. Chipo's Braids & Beauty, Saka's Barbershop and Mtendere Physio Studio are fictional businesses.

Contact

This demo does not list an email address or a phone number for PamoHub. Each sample business page shows the placeholder WhatsApp number +260 97 000 0000 and labels it demo number.

Terms

Nothing on this site is an offer to sell a service. Sample prices are examples. The pages send noindex.

Privacy

There is no account. A booking you make on a sample business page is stored in this browser's local storage. It is not sent to a server. Do not enter details you would not want saved on this device.

There are no PamoHub social media accounts in this demo. Handles on the sample business pages are placeholders, not live profiles.
