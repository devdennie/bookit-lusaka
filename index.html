One place to book

PamoHub is a campus-first place to discover local services and send a booking. Choose a campus, open a business, and pick a time.

How it works

  1. Discover

    Choose a campus and see the businesses listed there.

  2. Choose

    Pick a service and a time that fits.

  3. Book

    Add your name and phone, then review the details.

  4. Confirm

    Save the booking on this device and send it on WhatsApp when a number is listed.

Become a provider

Bring your business onto PamoHub. Provider registration opens with customer accounts.
